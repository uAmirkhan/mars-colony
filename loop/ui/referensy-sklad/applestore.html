BUILD YOUR DREAM TOWN! Welcome to Township, a thrilling city-building and farming game where you become the mayor of your own town! Build houses, factories, and community buildings, grow crops on your farm, and decorate your town just the way you like. Take part in limited-time events, compete in exciting regattas, and earn exclusive prizes! Need a break from city planning? Jump into relaxing match-3 puzzles to earn rewards, speed up your progress, and unlock even more fun - all available offline! Township - the perfect blend of city-building, farming, and match-3 gameplay! GAME FEATURES: ● Unlimited creativity: Design and build the metropolis of your dreams! ● Engaging match-3 puzzles: Complete fun levels to earn rewards and boost your progress! ● Exciting competitions: Test your skills against players from around the world in regular competitions - win prizes and make unforgettable memories! ● Exclusive collections: Gather valuable artifacts, rare antiquities, and colorful profile pictures to showcase your achievements! ● Offline play: Enjoy Township anytime, anywhere - even without an internet connection! ● Vibrant community: Meet friendly characters with unique personalities! ● Social connections: Play with your Facebook and Game Center friends or make new friends in the Township community! Why You'll Love Township: ● A unique mix of city-building, farming, and match-3 gameplay! ● Stunning graphics and charming animations ● Regular updates with fresh content and special events ● Personalize your town with a wide variety of decorations Township is free to play, but some in-game elements (including randomized items) can be purchased for real money. If you don't want to take advantage of this option, simply turn it off in your device's Restrictions menu.   • Land expansion & giving suggestions
    12/15/2020
    Lucky3momm

    Hi, I have just earned my 100 days in a row reward! I really enjoy playing the game and have picked up some of the ways to make it work without spending real cash. Not your goal, I know. But I took it as a personal challenge. I have a suggestion and went to your settings in the game to make my suggestion. It seems that you have suggestions shut off. I read a post from 2014, I think, which said I could post “right here” but no way to do that. Maybe you could update that post and suggest a real way to make suggestions. I went to Facebook, but no way to post there except in forums. I assume any suggestion there would be lost in the hundreds of daily posts. So here is my game suggestion.I love having space around my buildings and houses. I like to put in recreation areas and playgrounds. I also like to leave “wild” areas where the forest animals run around. However, I don’t like seeing all the $ signs on land where I can expand. It kind of ruins the look. I suggest you add either a “hide $” or “remove $” choice when available land is chosen. I would pay a small amount of town cash or maybe use a hammer to remove that sign. Then if I come back later, I would want to still have the option to expand and clear the land. I know I can expand and then spend town cash to buy lakes and trees, but really, that seems a waste. In fact I like the design of the wild land much better than what I can add myself. I hope this is doable. Thanks for considering it.
