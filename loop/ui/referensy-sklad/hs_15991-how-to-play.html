How to play?

Last Updated: 5h

Township is a game where you build your city, develop farms and factories, and complete match-3 levels.

One of the main goals is to send orders to earn coins and experience points.

To do this, you sow fields, take care of animals, and produce various goods at your factories.

Products from farms and factories are used to fulfill orders that you send using transport. In return, you receive resources to build up your city and expand its territory.

To get more resources, beat match-3 levels, take part in town events, and go on expeditions 🌾

🏙️How to develop the city?

To grow your city, you need to: Complete residents’ orders and send trains — they bring coins, experience, and building materials;

  • Use earned resources to build residential houses to increase the population and construct civic buildings;

  • The higher the population, the more factories you can build — which means you can send more orders.

Develop your city step by step — and it will become cozy and prosperous 🚜

🌍How to expand the city?

To expand your territory, you’ll need special tools and materials — for example, axes, saws, and dynamite. You can get them:

  • For completing orders;

  • In the mine;

  • As event rewards.

When you’ve collected the required tools, just tap a new area on the map to unlock it for construction 🏗️
