# 1. ДРОН — transport/drone.png, 512 по ширине
##############################################################

Урок шаттла применен здесь дословно: приземистость — правило для построек,
техника опознается силуэтом. У дрона обязаны читаться четыре признака:
кольцевые кожухи винтов, глаз-линза спереди, груз снизу, посадочные скобы.
Без них выходит не курьер, а летающая коробка.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

A single game asset sprite for a 2D mobile city-builder game, isolated on a solid pure
chromakey green #00FF00 background. Not an illustration, not a poster, not a concept
art scene: one game sprite, ready to be cut out and placed in a game.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES. These override everything else in this prompt.
1. NO OUTLINES. No contour line, no ink line, no comic linework, no dark edge stroke
   on any part of the object at any thickness. This is painted volume, not line art.
2. VOLUME, NOT FLAT FILL. Every surface carries a two-step gradient from a lighter
   lit facet into a darker shaded facet, with gentle ambient-occlusion darkening in
   deep seams only. No panel is a single flat tone.
3. NO CUBES, NO BOXES. Soft rounded volumes with very large corner radii: capsules,
   domes, cylinders with rounded caps. Nothing reads as a crate.
4. ONE OBJECT ONLY, centered, complete, nothing cropped at the frame edge. No second
   object, no sparkles, no stray highlights, no decorative flecks anywhere.
5. NO CAST SHADOW ON THE GROUND and NO GROUND AT ALL. The object floats alone on the
   flat green background. Self-shading on the object itself stays; the shadow it would
   throw does not. No green spill, no green reflection on the object itself.

Rendering language of Township and Hay Day: semi-gloss painted metal and ceramic
composite with a soft sheen, stylized toy-like light falloff, not physically realistic
lighting, the polished look of a premium mobile city-builder. A single soft key light
from the upper left at roughly 55 degrees, so the upper left facets are lighter and
the lower right facets are darker. Saturated painted colour, never washed out, never
pastel, never dusty.

Three-quarter view from slightly above, roughly 45 degrees.

The object: a friendly little courier drone of a Mars colony, hovering level, wider
than it is tall.

Body: a fat rounded capsule in warm cream-white, softly pillowed, with brass rivets
along a seam that runs around its middle. On top, a short rounded antenna nub and a
small teal beacon lamp.
Front, the face of the object: one large round lens in tinted teal glass set into a
brass ring, clearly reading as an eye. It is the only glass on the object.
Arms: four short thick arms sweeping out from the body, each ending in a full RING
housing around its rotor — a closed circular guard in muted terracotta, not a bare
propeller on a stick. The rotors inside sit still, no motion blur, no spin arcs.
Underneath, the most important part: a hexagonal cargo pod in warm amber with a brass
rim, held by two small rounded grippers, hanging clearly below the body so the drone
reads as carrying something.
Below the arms: four short rounded landing skids with soft pads.

Colours: warm cream-white body, muted terracotta rotor rings, warm amber cargo pod,
brass rims and rivets, tinted teal lens and beacon.

Avoid: military or surveillance drone styling, dark metals, camera gimbals, exposed
bare propellers, motion blur, speed lines, glow trails, exhaust, sparks, thin fragile
arms, boxy body, photorealistic quadcopter, sharp edges, any text or markings.

# 4. ГЕРОЙ-ПАНОРАМА КОЛОНИИ — 16:9, все механики в одном кадре
##############################################################

Это не ассет, а кадр для письма рекрутеру: одна картинка, по которой видно
всю игру. Поэтому в ней обязаны читаться все четыре механики сразу —
купол, шаттл со своей площадкой, дрон со своей площадкой, жилой квартал.

Композиция НАША, не перерисованная планировка Township. Референс Township
прикладывается ради плотности цвета и предметов, а не ради расстановки.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Match the colour density, prop density and rendering of the attached reference images,
but the composition and the subject are the Mars scene described below.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RENDER RULES. These override everything else in this prompt.
1. NO OUTLINES. No contour line, no ink line, no edge stroke, no dark rim on any
   object at any thickness. Shapes separate through colour and value only.
2. VOLUME, NOT FLAT FILL. Every surface carries a two-step gradient from a lighter
   lit facet into a darker shaded facet. No wall, roof or prop is a single flat tone.
3. NO CUBES, NO BOXES. Buildings are soft rounded volumes: barrel vaults, domes,
   capsules, cylinders with rounded caps, very large corner radii. Nothing reads as
   a shipping crate.
4. EVERY BUILDING A DIFFERENT SILHOUETTE. Never the same shell repeated in another
   colour.
5. ONE KEY LIGHT from the upper left at 55 degrees above the horizon. Every object
   drops a soft blurred shadow offset toward the lower right, extending past its own
   footprint. No near-vertical light, no shadow hidden under its object.

Soft volumetric shading baked into a 2D mobile game asset, in the rendering language
of Township and Hay Day. Stylized toy-like light falloff, not physically realistic
lighting. Finish is semi-gloss painted metal and ceramic composite with a soft sheen,
the polished look of a premium mobile city-builder.

A three-quarter view at roughly 45 degrees over a thriving Mars colony, moderate
camera distance so individual buildings and props stay readable. The horizon sits
about a quarter of the way down from the top, under a peach-pale sky.

CENTRE, the heart of the frame: two hydroponic domes of different diameters, true
hemispheres shaded with a smooth radial gradient, their frame ribs painted saturated
green-teal, dense emerald crop rows glowing through the glass as the most saturated
green in the whole frame. A rounded airlock porch at the base of the nearer dome,
painted tile ringing its base, a weather mast on a tripod beside the entrance.

LEFT: a shuttle landing pad — a low circular deck of ribbed metal in cream-white with
brass edging and soft-orange guide stripes around its rim, four short rounded corner
posts, one with a small radar dish. Standing on it, a chubby cargo shuttle lying
horizontally, roughly twice as long as it is tall, cream-white hull with brass trim,
a wide curved teal cockpit windscreen at its lifted nose, two short rounded stabiliser
fins and two round nozzles at the rear, a row of four recessed cargo hatch doors along
its side painted soft terracotta with brass frames and latch handles — doors, never
round windows. Cargo pallets and a coiled fuel hose on a reel beside the pad.

RIGHT FOREGROUND: a small hexagonal drone pad — a squat six-sided cream-white pedestal
with brass corner strips, a shallow teal cradle on top and a slim mast with an amber
beacon. Just above it, a little courier drone hovers level: a fat rounded cream-white
capsule body, one round teal lens at the front, four short arms ending in closed
terracotta ring housings around the rotors, a hexagonal amber cargo pod slung
underneath. A short stack of small parcel containers striped in terracotta and cream
sits against the pedestal base.

BACKGROUND RIGHT: a residential row of four habitat modules, each a clearly different
rounded shape, no two alike — a brick red barrel vault, a mustard yellow two-storey
shell with a domed roof cap, a terracotta low capsule with a curved porch canopy, a
teal blue upright cylinder with a ring walkway. Every module has a coloured lamp beside
its door. Behind them, a field of solar panels with a cold blue-grey sheen of glass.

CONNECTING THE SCENE: painted paths run between all four zones, edged by long planter
troughs of muted sage-green moss behind a low glass rim, with a partial transparent
canopy over one stretch. A teal coolant tank with a level gauge stands beside the
domes, and a thin glazed ground pipe carrying visibly bluish fluid runs from it across
the ground. Six colonists in light pastel suits walk the paths, two of them loading a
pallet by the shuttle.

Every building is a separate standalone structure spaced apart on open ground with
clear air and visible ground around it, never fused into a shared mass, never joined
by tube corridors or connecting walkways, linked only by thin ground-level pipes and
painted paths. Every structure is squat, height roughly equal to its own footprint
width, with large rounded corners and no sharp edges.

At least four colour families are visible at once and exactly one of them is cool
teal-cyan; no family except the sky covers more than 40 percent of the frame. Painted
structures and decorative props read clearly more saturated than the sky and the ground
behind them, by a wide and obvious margin. Pure clean blue never appears on any world
object.

Ground: warm ochre sand as the base, dark basalt gravel on the industrial apron by the
landing pad, a paler trodden dust path, a rust-red dust band at the far edge of the
built area, painted tile at the dome entrances. Sky is peach-pale Martian daytime
graduating to muted blue-grey near the zenith, occupying only the top quarter of the
frame and never spilling below the horizon line.

Mood: sunny midday, a prosperous lived-in colony, calm optimism, a place where you
would want to stay.

NEGATIVE:
any outline or contour line on world objects, thick black outline, comic ink line;
single flat color fill with no gradient anywhere on an object;
hard-edged two-tone shading with a sharp light/shadow cutoff;
boxy angular crate buildings, square-cornered domes, identical repeated modules;
monochrome beige blob, whole scene at one flat lightness, everything cream and ochre;
cream or sand-white hull on every building, unpainted residential blocks;
moss or plants growing loose in open ground with no glass rim or canopy;
pure clean blue on world objects;
yellow-and-black hazard hatching, warning stripes, alarm markings, radiation symbols;
sky filling half the frame, sky spilling below the horizon line;
one single flat ground material across the whole frame;
near-vertical overhead light, shadow hidden directly under its object;
objects floating with no visible offset shadow;
flat 2D vector illustration, sticker-sheet look, infographic icon set, paper cutout;
buildings fused into one mass, joined by tube corridors, shared walls;
tall tower-like vertically stretched structures;
empty bare ground between buildings;
upright rocket pose, round portholes along the shuttle side, flame, exhaust, smoke;
exposed bare propellers, motion blur, speed lines, glow trails on the drone;
photorealistic lighting, long sunset shadows, dust storm, craters, canyons, cracks,
ruins, abandonment;
blue Earth sky, dark starry sky, text, UI overlay
