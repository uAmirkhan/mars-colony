NON-NEGOTIABLE RENDER RULES. These override everything else in this prompt.
1. NO OUTLINES. No contour line, no ink line, no edge stroke, no dark rim on any object

2. VOLUME, NOT FLAT FILL. Every surface carries a two-step gradient from a lighter lit

3. NO CUBES, NO BOXES. Every building is a soft rounded volume with very large corner
   radii: barrel vaults, true hemispheres, capsules, cylinders with rounded caps. Not
   one structure reads as a shipping crate or a rounded-off box.
4. EVERY BUILDING A DIFFERENT SILHOUETTE. No shell is ever repeated in another colour.
5. ONE KEY LIGHT from the upper left at 55 degrees above the horizon. Every object —
   dome, module, shuttle, drone, tank, crate, colonist — casts a soft blurred shadow

6. BUILDINGS STAND APART. Every structure is a separate standalone object with clear
   air and visible ground around it. Never fused into a shared mass, never joined by
   tube corridors, never sharing walls. They are linked only by thin ground-level
