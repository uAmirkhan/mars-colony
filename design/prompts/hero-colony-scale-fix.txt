================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Keep this exact Mars colony scene: same layout, same buildings, same zones, same
lighting, same style, same camera. Change only the scale of two things.

CHANGE 1: THE SHUTTLE IS FAR TOO BIG.
Shrink the shuttle and its round landing pad to about one third of their current
size. The shuttle should read as a cargo vehicle roughly two housing domes long,
comfortably smaller than the nearby buildings, not the biggest object in the frame.
Keep its shape, its cream and orange colours and its landing pad, just much smaller,
in the same spot. Fill the sand freed up around it with ground detail that already
exists elsewhere in the scene: crates, small rovers, a few colonists, cargo pallets,
rocks and wheel tracks.

CHANGE 2: THE DRONE IS HARD TO NOTICE.
Do not make the drone much bigger. It stays a small vehicle, far smaller than the
shuttle. Make it READABLE instead: lift the drone into the air, hovering just above
its landing pad, carrying a small cargo container slung underneath, with a soft
blurred shadow on the pad below it. Give it a bright accent colour and small glowing
lights on its rotors so the eye catches it. Its hexagonal pad must look clearly
different from the shuttle's round pad. Add one or two colonists and a couple of
small cargo boxes beside the pad so it reads as a working delivery point.

Everything else stays exactly as it is.
