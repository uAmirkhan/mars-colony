ПРО СТЕКЛО, ГЛАВНОЕ. Прошлая версия купола была отвергнута именно из-за него:
стекло вышло по-настоящему прозрачным, и после съема фона на его месте будет
дыра или серая вуаль. В промпте ниже стекло прямо потребовано закрашенным
непрозрачным тоном, а грядки — сплошным цветом сквозь него. Пока это условие
держится, хватает обычного зеленого экрана, и возня с дублем на белом и черном
не нужна. Если модель все равно нарисует настоящее стекло — тогда дубль,
и второй кадр делается РЕДАКТИРОВАНИЕМ первого, а не новой генерацией.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

A single game asset sprite for a 2D mobile city-builder game, isolated on a solid pure
chromakey green #00FF00 background. Not an illustration, not a poster, not a concept
art scene: one game sprite, ready to be cut out and placed in a game.

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES. These override everything else in this prompt.
1. NO OUTLINES. No contour line, no ink line, no comic linework, no dark edge stroke
   on any part of the object at any thickness. This is painted volume, not line art.
2. VOLUME, NOT FLAT FILL. Every surface carries a two-step gradient from a lighter
   lit facet into a darker shaded facet, with gentle ambient-occlusion darkening in
   deep seams only. No panel is a single flat tone.
3. NO CUBES, NO BOXES. Soft rounded volumes with very large corner radii. Nothing
   reads as a crate or an angular greenhouse.
4. ONE OBJECT ONLY, centered, complete, nothing cropped at the frame edge. No second
   building, no piece of a neighbour, no cut-off figure, no sparkles, no stray
   highlights, no decorative flecks anywhere in the frame.
5. NO CAST SHADOW ON THE GROUND and NO GROUND AT ALL. The object stands alone on the
   flat green background. Self-shading on the object itself stays; the shadow it would
   throw does not. No green spill, no green reflection on the object itself.

Rendering language of Township and Hay Day: semi-gloss painted metal and ceramic
composite with a soft sheen, stylized toy-like light falloff, not physically realistic
lighting, the polished look of a premium mobile city-builder. A single soft key light
from the upper left at roughly 55 degrees, so the upper left facets are lighter and
the lower right facets are darker. Saturated painted colour, clearly not washed out,
never pastel, never dusty.

Squat proportions, height roughly equal to footprint width. Three-quarter view from
slightly above, roughly 45 degrees.

The object: a hydroponic dome of a Mars colony.

Shape: a TRUE HEMISPHERE, a clean half-sphere sitting on a wide base ring, shaded with
a smooth radial gradient — brightest near the crown, darker toward the base rim. Never
a boxy angular greenhouse, never a faceted geodesic cage, never a square-cornered
structure.

Shell: a pale blue-green shell held by a frame of curved ribs painted a saturated
green-teal, the ribs running from the crown down to the base ring like meridians, each
rib a rounded raised strip with its own light and shade, never a drawn line.

Interior, the most important part: through the shell, dense rows of vivid emerald crop
beds are clearly visible on two stepped tiers — the most saturated green of the whole
object. The shell is PAINTED, NOT TRANSPARENT: the crops read as solid opaque colour
seen through a tinted pane, the silhouette of the dome stays clean and filled, and no
part of the object is see-through or glassy enough to show the background behind it.

Base: a wide rounded base ring in warm cream with brass trim. At the front of the ring
sits a small rounded airlock porch with a saturated teal frame and a chunky brass door
handle. A ring of painted tile runs around the base and ends exactly at the footprint,
going no wider.

Pressed against the base, part of the same sprite: a low planter trough of muted
sage-green moss behind a low glass rim, clearly duller than the emerald crops inside
the dome, and a slim weather mast on a tripod with a small brass dish.

Colours: pale blue-green shell, saturated green-teal ribs and airlock frame, vivid
emerald crops inside, warm cream base ring, brass trim, muted sage moss outside.

Avoid: any outline or contour line, ink line, comic linework, dark edge stroke; flat
single-tone fill with no gradient; a boxy angular greenhouse, a faceted geodesic dome,
a square-cornered structure; genuinely transparent or see-through glass, background
visible through the shell, a hollow or empty dome; a cast shadow on the ground, any
ground, any base plate, any platform under the object; green spill or green reflection
on the object; a second building, a cropped neighbour, colonist figures, sparkles,
lens flare, decorative flecks; yellow-and-black hazard hatching, warning stripes,
radiation symbols; dark metals, soot, rust, cracks, dust, wear; photorealistic
lighting, long sunset shadows; text, numbers, labels, UI overlay.
