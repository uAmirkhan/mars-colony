под стройку, и делают колонию живой. Но кнопок за ними нет, и если о кадре
зайдет разговор, называть их антуражем, а не механикой.

колонии вообще, а не станок, производящий панели и каркасы.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Edit the attached image of a Mars colony. Keep the existing composition, the camera
angle, the colour palette and the overall look. Keep every building that is already
there exactly where it is. Do not move, resize or restyle the two hydroponic domes, the
shuttle on its round landing pad, the courier drone above its hexagonal pedestal pad,
the warehouse, the food module, the painted habitat modules, the ice quarry or the
regolith drill rig. Only apply the corrections and additions listed below.

PART ONE — CORRECTIONS TO WHAT IS ALREADY THERE.

1. REMOVE ALL OUTLINES. Every object currently carries a dark brown contour line around
   its silhouette and around its internal details. Remove every one of them completely.
   No contour line, no ink line, no edge stroke, no dark rim anywhere, at any thickness,
   on any object — buildings, vehicles, props, colonists, ground features. Objects must
   separate from each other purely through colour and value contrast, the way a painted
   volume does. This is the single most important correction in this list.

2. ADD VOLUME TO EVERY SURFACE. Most walls, roofs and props are currently filled with a
   single flat tone. Repaint each surface with a two-step gradient: a lighter lit facet
   flowing into a darker shaded facet, plus a gentle darkening in deep seams and where
   two surfaces meet. Domes keep a smooth radial gradient, brightest near the crown and
   darker toward the base rim. Cylinders and barrel vaults get a soft band of light
   along their upper left curve fading into shade on the lower right. No panel, no wall
   and no roof stays a single flat tone.

3. ADD CAST SHADOWS. There is a single key light coming from the upper left at roughly
   55 degrees above the horizon. Every object — every dome, module, habitat, the
   shuttle, the drone, every tank, crate, container, rover, mast, planter and colonist —
   casts one soft blurred shadow on the ground, offset toward the lower right and
   visibly extending past its own footprint, so nothing looks pasted onto the sand. The
   drone, hovering, casts its shadow on the ground below and to the lower right of it.

4. REMOVE the bright sparkle or lens flare in the lower right corner of the frame. No
   sparkles, no glints, no decorative flecks anywhere in the image.

PART TWO — FIVE NEW STRUCTURES TO ADD.

Place them in the open sand and gravel areas between and behind the existing buildings.
Each is a separate standalone structure with clear air and visible ground around it,
never touching or fused with its neighbours, linked to the rest only by thin

the frame. All of them are squat, roughly as tall as they are wide, with large rounded
corners and no sharp edges, in the same painted semi-gloss style as the existing
buildings.

  1. Обводки нет ни на одном объекте. Осталась хоть где-то — правка провалена,

  3. Каждый объект отбрасывает мягкую тень вправо-вниз, выходящую за габарит.

  4. Все четырнадцать узлов на месте и узнаются по отдельности.
  5. Стройплощадка читается как стройка: недостроенный корпус, кран, штабели

  6. Космопорт лайнера не спутан с площадкой шаттла: длинный перрон с вышкой
     против маленького круглого диска.
  7. Площадка дрона по-прежнему шестиугольная тумба, а не второй диск.

  9. Купол остается самым насыщенным зеленым объектом.
 10. Сигнальной раскраски опасности нет нигде.
