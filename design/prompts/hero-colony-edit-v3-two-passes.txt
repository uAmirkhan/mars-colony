ДВА ПРОХОДА ПРАВКИ: КОСМОПОРТ, ЗАТЕМ СТРОЙПЛОЩАДКА.

ПОЧЕМУ ПО ОДНОМУ, А НЕ СПИСКОМ. Прогон v2 просил пять построек разом. Дошли
три мелких и средних — текстильный модуль, атмосферный, энергостанция. Не
дошли ровно две самые крупные и составные: космопорт с башней и краном,
стройплощадка с недостроем и штабелями. Правка охотно дописывает локальную
деталь и плохо строит большое сооружение, когда оно идет четвертым пунктом
списка. Лечится узостью запроса, а не усилением слов.

ЧЕГО В ЭТИХ ПРОМПТАХ НАМЕРЕННО НЕТ. Требований убрать обводку, добавить объем
и тени. Три захода подряд их не взяли, а лишние требования размывают главный
пункт. Держим инструкцию узкой: один объект плюс немного мелочи.

РЕЖИМ: редактирование приложенной картинки, не новая генерация.
ПОРЯДОК: сначала проход 1, к проходу 2 прикладывается результат прохода 1.

# ПРОХОД 1 — КОСМОПОРТ ЛАЙНЕРА
##############################################################

Идет первым, потому что это самое крупное сооружение кадра и ему нужно
свободное место в фоне. После стройплощадки места уже не останется.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Edit the attached image of a Mars colony. Keep the composition, the camera angle, the
colour palette and every existing building exactly as they are. Add ONE new structure
and a few small props. Change nothing else.

THE ONE NEW STRUCTURE: an interplanetary liner spaceport, placed in the open background
behind and beyond the round shuttle landing pad, further from the camera than everything
else, so it reads as the edge of the colony. It is the largest structure in the picture
and it must look like a completely different kind of place from the small round shuttle
pad in the foreground.

Build it from these parts:

A long wide apron of ribbed metal decking with softly rounded corners and brass edging,
raised on a low rounded rim, laid out lengthwise across the background. Its surface
carries soft-orange guide lines painted flat onto the decking — decorative painted
lines, never hazard hatching, never yellow-and-black stripes.

Standing at one end of the apron, a rounded control tower in warm cream-white, the only
tall object in the picture: a smooth cylinder with brass bands around it, widening near
the top into a wide observation ring glazed in tinted teal glass, capped by a softly
domed roof. On the roof, a radar antenna dish turned to one side and a small blue-green
beacon lamp at the tip. The tower is rounded and friendly, never a sharp angular mast,
never a military control tower.

Along the far edge of the apron, two long rows of numbered cargo containers stacked two
high, striped in terracotta, mustard and cream, each container a rounded box with brass
corner fittings. Beside them, a rounded loading crane in cream-white with a soft-orange
boom, standing on short rails, lifting one container by a brass hook.

The apron stands ready and EMPTY: no liner ship, no rocket, no spacecraft of any kind
parked on it.

The spaceport is a separate standalone structure with clear open ground around it. It
does not touch, overlap or fuse with the shuttle pad, the warehouse or anything else
already in the picture.

ALSO ADD, small and easy:

A wide painted path in warm cream running from the spaceport apron across the sand
toward the warehouse and the shuttle pad, joining the colony's existing paths, with a
thin ground-level pipe running beside it.

Four more small colonists in light pastel suits: two walking together along the new
path, one guiding a loaded cart toward the containers, one standing beside the crane
looking up at it. Draw them the same size and in the same style as the colonists
already in the picture.

A line of small route flag markers on thin masts along the edge of the new path.

KEEP EVERYTHING ELSE UNCHANGED: the two hydroponic domes, the shuttle on its round pad,
the drone above its hexagonal pad, the habitat modules, the warehouse, the food module,
the textile module, the atmosphere module, the power station, the ice quarry and the
drill rig all stay exactly where they are, at their current size and colour.

AVOID: any liner or rocket standing on the apron, yellow-and-black hazard hatching,
warning markings, military airfield styling, dark metals, soot, scorch marks, sharp
angular towers, overhead cables strung through the air, text, numbers, labels, signage
lettering, sparkles, lens flare.

================ ДО СЮДА ================

ПРОВЕРИТЬ ПОСЛЕ ПРОХОДА 1:
  - Космопорт появился и стоит в фоне, а не поверх существующих построек.
  - Башня читается как башня и это единственный высокий объект кадра.
  - Космопорт не спутан с площадкой шаттла: длинный перрон против круглого диска.
  - Ни одна старая постройка не поехала, не поменяла цвет и не исчезла.
  - На перроне нет корабля.

# ПРОХОД 2 — СТРОЙПЛОЩАДКА В РАБОТЕ
##############################################################

Прикладывается результат прохода 1. Это второй по сложности объект и он несет
механику стройки, которой на кадре нет вообще.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Edit the attached image of a Mars colony. Keep the composition, the camera angle, the
colour palette and every existing building exactly as they are. Add ONE new scene and a
few small props. Change nothing else.

THE ONE NEW SCENE: a building site under construction, placed in an open sandy area
between the row of habitat modules and the hydroponic domes, standing apart from both
with clear ground around it. It must read instantly as a half-finished building with
work going on, not as a finished structure.

Build it from these parts:

A rounded foundation platform of painted pale concrete with a brass rim, sitting flat on
the sand.

On the platform, a new habitat module HALF BUILT: its lower half is already a finished
curved shell in saturated mustard yellow with a brass base band and one rounded window
in place. Its upper half is still an open skeleton of rounded cream-white ribs curving up
and meeting at the top, with the sky-side gaps between the ribs empty, so the shape it
will become is obvious and the building is clearly unfinished. One curved wall panel is
already fitted between two ribs; the rest are still open.

Beside the platform, a small rounded crane in cream-white with a soft-orange boom and a
brass hook, holding one more curved mustard wall panel in the air just above the gap it
will fill.

Around the base of the platform, neat stacks of building materials, each stack a
different colour and shape so they read as different materials: a bundle of flat cream
panels tied with brass straps, a rack of curved cream-white ribs standing on end, three
rolls of teal sealant sheeting, a crate of pale filters, and two coils of coloured
cable. They are chunky rounded objects, never flat boxes.

A low decorative barrier of short rounded posts joined by soft-orange rope rings the
site. Decorative rope only, never hazard tape, never yellow-and-black striping.

Three small colonists in light pastel suits work at the site: one on the platform
guiding the hanging panel with a raised hand, one at the material stacks lifting a rib,
one standing back holding a rolled plan. Draw them the same size and in the same style
as the colonists already in the picture.

ALSO ADD, small and easy:

A short painted path in warm cream connecting the building site to the nearest existing
path, with a thin ground-level pipe running beside it.

Two more small colonists walking together elsewhere on the existing paths, and one
sitting on the bench that is already in the picture, so the colony looks busier.

KEEP EVERYTHING ELSE UNCHANGED: the two hydroponic domes, the shuttle on its round pad,
the drone above its hexagonal pad, the habitat modules, the warehouse, the food module,
the textile module, the atmosphere module, the power station, the liner spaceport with
its control tower, the ice quarry and the drill rig all stay exactly where they are, at
their current size and colour.

AVOID: a finished completed building on the platform, scaffolding poles and lattice
towers, yellow-and-black hazard tape, warning signs, safety cones, rubble, dust clouds,
debris, a construction pit or excavation, dark metals, rust, text, numbers, labels,
sparkles, lens flare.

================ ДО СЮДА ================

ПРОВЕРИТЬ ПОСЛЕ ПРОХОДА 2:
  - Здание читается как НЕДОСТРОЕННОЕ: верх открытый каркас из ребер.
    Вышло готовое здание — узел потерян, механика стройки снова не показана.
  - Кран есть и он держит панель в воздухе, а не стоит просто так.
  - Штабели материалов различимы между собой по цвету и форме.
  - Ни одна старая постройка не поехала и не исчезла, космопорт на месте.

# ЕСЛИ ОБЪЕКТ СНОВА НЕ ПОЯВИТСЯ
##############################################################

Значит правка не тянет постройку такого размера в принципе. Тогда обходной путь:
генерировать космопорт и стройплощадку ОТДЕЛЬНЫМИ АССЕТАМИ на зеленом фоне по
правилам art-prompts-assets.md, а собирать их в общий кадр коллажем — это все
равно придется делать для игры, потому что в билде каждый объект отдельный файл.
Герой-кадр тогда собирается из принятых спрайтов, а не догенерируется целиком.
