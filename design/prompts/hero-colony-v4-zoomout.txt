РАСШИРЕНИЕ ХОЛСТА И ТРИ ПРОХОДА ПОСЛЕ НЕГО.

ЧТО СЛУЧИЛОСЬ НА ПРОШЛОМ ПРОГОНЕ. Промпт просил добавить космопорт, не трогая
остальное. Модель космопорт нарисовала, но свободного места под него в кадре
не было — и она освободила его сносом: пропали текстильный модуль, атмосферный
модуль и почти весь жилой ряд. Инструкция «keep everything else unchanged»
проиграла физике кадра. Если места нет, правка не добавляет, а замещает.

ВЫВОД. Сначала расширяем холст и получаем пустой грунт по краям. Только потом
дорисовываем в него крупные объекты. Порядок обратный тому, что мы делали.

КАКУЮ КАРТИНКУ БРАТЬ ЗА ОСНОВУ. Предыдущую — ту, где на месте текстильный
модуль с катушкой нити, атмосферный модуль, энергостанция и весь жилой ряд,
а космопорта еще нет. НЕ последнюю: в ней потеряно слишком много.

ТРИ ПРОХОДА, СТРОГО ПО ОЧЕРЕДИ:
  1. Отдалить камеру, дорисовать пустой грунт по краям. Ничего не добавлять.
  2. Космопорт лайнера в новой пустой зоне.
  3. Стройплощадка в новой пустой зоне.
Каждый следующий проход получает результат предыдущего.

# ПРОХОД 1 — ОТДАЛИТЬ КАМЕРУ, НИЧЕГО НЕ ДОБАВЛЯТЬ
##############################################################

Самый важный и самый скучный проход. Единственная его задача — получить
свободное место. Если на нем что-то появится сверх грунта, следующие проходы
опять начнут вытеснять постройки.

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Zoom out from the attached image of a Mars colony. Extend the picture outward on all
four sides, so the existing colony ends up smaller and centred, surrounded by a wide
generous band of new open Martian ground.

THE EXISTING COLONY IS FROZEN. Every building, vehicle, prop, path and figure already
in the picture stays exactly as it is: same shapes, same colours, same positions
relative to each other, same style. Nothing is moved, redrawn, restyled, removed or
replaced. The whole existing scene is simply reproduced smaller inside the new, larger
frame. Do not redesign anything. Do not add anything inside the existing colony.

THE NEW OUTER BAND contains only empty terrain, and it must be wide — the existing
colony should end up occupying roughly the middle half of the new picture, with clearly
open, buildable ground all the way around it. NO NEW BUILDINGS, NO NEW STRUCTURES, NO
VEHICLES anywhere in the new band. It stays empty on purpose: it is the space where
buildings will be added later.

Fill the new band with ground and landscape only, continuing what is already there:
warm ochre sand as the base, patches of dark basalt gravel, a paler trodden dust area,
a rust-red dust band toward the outer edge, a few scattered rounded boulders and small
stone clusters, and low gentle rises. Continue the existing painted paths a short way
outward so they trail off into the open sand, and continue one thin ground-level pipe
the same way.

Along the upper edge of the new picture, add a distant horizon: a soft band of low
rounded hills in pale rosy-grey haze, and above them a peach-pale Martian daytime sky
graduating to muted blue-grey higher up. The sky occupies only the top fifth of the new
picture and never spills below the horizon line. This is the only new element allowed
besides ground.

Keep the same rendering style, the same lighting direction and the same colour palette
as the existing image, so the new ground reads as the same picture, not as a border
pasted around it.

AVOID: any new building, dome, tower, pad, vehicle, rover, drone or machine in the new
outer band; craters, canyons, cracks, chasms, ruins, wreckage; dust storm, dark clouds,
night, stars; changing the size, colour, position or design of anything in the existing
colony; a visible seam, frame or border between the old picture and the new ground;
text, numbers, labels, watermark.

================ ДО СЮДА ================

ПРОВЕРИТЬ ПОСЛЕ ПРОХОДА 1:
  - Старая колония цела: текстильный модуль с катушкой, атмосферный, энергостанция,
    весь жилой ряд, купола, шаттл, дрон, склад, карьер, буровая — все на месте.
  - Вокруг колонии реально много пустого грунта, не узкая кайма.
  - В новой зоне НЕТ ни одной постройки.
  - Шва между старой картинкой и новым грунтом не видно.
  - Сверху появился горизонт и небо примерно на пятую часть кадра.

Если старая часть поехала или что-то пропало — повторить проход, усилив первую
фразу: попросить воспроизвести существующую сцену без единого изменения.

# ПРОХОД 2 — КОСМОПОРТ ЛАЙНЕРА В ПУСТОЙ ЗОНЕ
##############################################################

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Edit the attached image of a Mars colony. Add ONE new structure, placed entirely in the
EMPTY OPEN GROUND in the upper right area of the picture, well away from the existing
buildings. Do not place it on top of anything. Do not move, resize, restyle or remove
any existing building, vehicle, prop, path or figure. There is open sand available —
use it.

THE ONE NEW STRUCTURE: an interplanetary liner spaceport, the largest structure in the
picture, standing at the edge of the colony and clearly a different kind of place from
the small round shuttle pad.

A long wide apron of ribbed metal decking with softly rounded corners and brass edging,
raised on a low rounded rim, laid out lengthwise in the empty ground. Its surface carries
soft-orange guide lines painted flat onto the decking — decorative painted lines, never
hazard hatching, never yellow-and-black stripes.

At one end of the apron stands a rounded control tower in warm cream-white: a smooth
cylinder with brass bands, widening near the top into a wide observation ring glazed in
tinted teal glass, capped by a softly domed roof, with a radar dish and a small
blue-green beacon on top. Rounded and friendly, never a sharp angular mast.

Along the far edge of the apron, two rows of numbered cargo containers stacked two high,
striped in terracotta, mustard and cream, each a rounded box with brass corner fittings.
Beside them a rounded loading crane in cream-white with a soft-orange boom on short
rails, lifting one container by a brass hook.

The apron stands ready and EMPTY: no liner, no rocket, no spacecraft parked on it.

A wide painted path in warm cream runs from the apron across the open sand to join the
colony's existing paths, with a thin ground-level pipe beside it and a line of small
route flags on thin masts along its edge. Four small colonists in light pastel suits
work around the apron: two walking the new path, one guiding a loaded cart, one beside
the crane. Same size and style as the colonists already in the picture.

AVOID: placing the spaceport on top of or overlapping any existing building; removing,
shrinking or recolouring anything that is already in the picture; a liner or rocket on
the apron; yellow-and-black hazard hatching, warning markings, military airfield styling,
dark metals, soot, scorch marks, sharp angular towers, overhead cables strung through
the air; text, numbers, labels, sparkles, lens flare.

================ ДО СЮДА ================

ПРОВЕРИТЬ ПОСЛЕ ПРОХОДА 2:
  - Космопорт стоит в пустой зоне, а не поверх старых построек.
  - Текстильный модуль, атмосферный, энергостанция и жилой ряд ЦЕЛЫ.
    Это главный пункт: именно они гибли в прошлый раз.
  - Башня читается как башня, перрон не спутан с круглым диском шаттла.
  - На перроне нет корабля.

# ПРОХОД 3 — СТРОЙПЛОЩАДКА В ПУСТОЙ ЗОНЕ
##############################################################

================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

Edit the attached image of a Mars colony. Add ONE new scene, placed entirely in the
EMPTY OPEN GROUND in the lower left area of the picture, well away from the existing
buildings. Do not place it on top of anything. Do not move, resize, restyle or remove
any existing building, vehicle, prop, path or figure.

THE ONE NEW SCENE: a building site under construction. It must read instantly as a
half-finished building with work going on, not as a finished structure.

A rounded foundation platform of painted pale concrete with a brass rim sits flat on the
sand. On it, a new habitat module HALF BUILT: its lower half is a finished curved shell
in saturated mustard yellow with a brass base band and one rounded window in place; its
upper half is still an open skeleton of rounded cream-white ribs curving up and meeting
at the top, the gaps between the ribs empty so the unfinished shape is obvious. One
curved wall panel is already fitted between two ribs, the rest are still open.

Beside the platform, a small rounded crane in cream-white with a soft-orange boom and a
brass hook holds one more curved mustard panel in the air, just above the gap it will
fill.

Around the base, neat stacks of building materials, each a different colour and shape so
they read as different materials: a bundle of flat cream panels tied with brass straps,
a rack of curved cream-white ribs standing on end, three rolls of teal sealant sheeting,
a crate of pale filters, two coils of coloured cable. Chunky rounded objects, never flat
boxes.

A low decorative barrier of short rounded posts joined by soft-orange rope rings the
site — decorative rope only, never hazard tape, never yellow-and-black striping.

Three small colonists in light pastel suits work there: one on the platform guiding the
hanging panel with a raised hand, one lifting a rib at the stacks, one standing back
with a rolled plan. Same size and style as the colonists already in the picture.

A short painted path in warm cream connects the site to the nearest existing path, with
a thin ground-level pipe beside it.

AVOID: a finished completed building on the platform; placing the site on top of or
overlapping anything existing; removing, shrinking or recolouring anything already in
the picture; scaffolding poles and lattice towers, hazard tape, warning signs, safety
cones, rubble, debris, dust clouds, an excavation pit, dark metals, rust; text, numbers,
labels, sparkles, lens flare.

================ ДО СЮДА ================

ПРОВЕРИТЬ ПОСЛЕ ПРОХОДА 3:
  - Здание НЕДОСТРОЕНО: верх — открытый каркас из ребер. Вышло готовое здание —
    механика стройки снова не показана, узел потерян.
  - Кран держит панель в воздухе, а не стоит просто так.
  - Космопорт с прошлого прохода цел, старые постройки целы.

# ЕСЛИ ПОСТРОЙКИ ПРОДОЛЖАТ ГИБНУТЬ ПРИ КАЖДОЙ ПРАВКЕ
##############################################################

Значит редактирование целого кадра для нас потолок, и дальше идем коллажем:
каждый объект генерируется отдельным спрайтом на зеленом фоне по правилам
art-prompts-assets.md, а сцена собирается из принятых спрайтов. Это все равно
предстоит для билда — там каждый объект отдельный файл, и сцену собирает код.
Тогда герой-кадр перестает быть отдельной работой и становится побочным
продуктом готовых ассетов.
