================ КОПИРОВАТЬ ОТСЮДА ================

You are given two images of the same Mars colony game world, drawn in the same style.

IMAGE 1 is the BASE. IMAGE 2 is the DONOR.

Your task: reproduce IMAGE 1 exactly as it is, and add into it three specific objects
copied from IMAGE 2. Nothing else changes.

=== RULE ONE: IMAGE 1 IS FROZEN ===

Every single thing in IMAGE 1 stays exactly as it is: the two green hydroponic domes, the
cargo shuttle on its round landing pad, the courier drone above its hexagonal pedestal
pad, the warehouse, the food module with its striped awning, the purple textile module,
the teal atmosphere module, the power station, the ice pool at the top left, the water
tank, the paths, the planter troughs, the benches, the crates, the pipes and every
colonist. Same shapes, same colours, same positions, same sizes, same style, same
lighting. Do not redesign, restyle, move, resize, recolour or remove anything. Do not
"improve" anything. The base image is approved and finished; you are only adding to it.

=== RULE TWO: TAKE EXACTLY THREE OBJECTS FROM IMAGE 2 ===

Copy these three objects out of IMAGE 2 and place them into the open empty sand of IMAGE
1. Redraw each one at the scale, camera angle and lighting of IMAGE 1 so it belongs there,
but keep its design, its colours and its details exactly as they are in IMAGE 2.

OBJECT A — THE BUILDING SITE UNDER CONSTRUCTION.
In IMAGE 2 it is the large golden-yellow dome that is only half built. Take the whole
scene around it: the rounded foundation platform, the finished lower part of the dome in
saturated mustard yellow, the OPEN SKELETON of cream-white ribs curving up above it with
open air between them so the dome is obviously unfinished, the small orange-and-cream
crane standing beside it holding a curved panel in the air, the stacks of building
materials around the base — the tied bundle of flat panels, the rack of curved ribs, the
rolls of sealant, the coils of cable, the tool chest — and the low boundary of short posts
joined by a soft-orange rope. Place it in the open sand in the LOWER LEFT area of the
base image, standing clear of everything already there.

OBJECT B — THE ICE QUARRY WITH ITS CUTTING MACHINE.
IMAGE 1 already has a pale ice pool at its top left, but nothing is happening in it.
Take from IMAGE 2 the working machinery and put it into that existing ice pool: the small
rounded cutting machine on fat wheels with its drum head, sitting down inside the pit on
the ice and cutting a fresh block, several cut ice blocks as pale translucent cubes with
rounded corners stacked on the ice beside it, and one or two colonists working at the
edge. Keep the existing pool shape and the existing pump building of IMAGE 1 as they are;
only add the machine, the ice blocks and the workers into it.

OBJECT C — THE DRILL RIG WITH ITS CONVEYOR AND WHITE ROVER.
In IMAGE 2 it stands on dark gravel at the upper right. Take the whole group: the rounded
drill rig with its open derrick and mustard collar, the conveyor belt on rounded drums
carrying rust-red ore lumps up to the ore hoppers, the hoppers themselves with one heaped
full of rust-red ore, the small white rover with its teal cabin parked underneath with a
half-loaded bed, the colour-coded canisters and the workers beside it. Place it in the
open sand in the UPPER RIGHT area of the base image, on a patch of dark basalt gravel,
standing clear of everything already there.

=== RULE THREE: WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR ===

Do NOT bring the long tube-like rail on pylons from IMAGE 2. No launch rail, no
accelerator, no row of rings, no bridge, no elevated tube, no pipeline on legs, no
viaduct. That object is deliberately excluded.

Do not bring anything else from IMAGE 2: not its buildings, not its ground, not its sky,
not its paths, not its layout. Only the three objects listed above.

=== RULE FOUR: MAKE THE ADDITIONS BELONG ===

Each added object stands alone on the sand with clear air and visible ground around it,
touching and overlapping nothing. Each casts a soft blurred shadow offset toward the
lower right, matching the lighting of the base image, which comes from the upper left.
Connect each one to the colony with a short painted cream path and a thin ground-level
pipe running along the sand, in the same style as the paths already in the base image.

=== NEGATIVE ===

a long rail, tube, bridge, viaduct, pipeline on pylons or row of rings anywhere;
a finished completed building on the construction foundation;
any change to the shuttle, the drone, the domes, the habitat modules, the factory
buildings, the power station or the pump building of the base image;
new buildings other than the three objects listed;
yellow tracked excavators, earth-moving diggers, bulldozers, real-world construction
machinery;
radiation symbols, yellow-and-black hazard hatching, warning tape, safety cones, alarm
markings;
craters, canyons, cracks, rubble, debris, wreckage, ruins;
any outline, contour line, ink line or dark edge stroke on any object;
flat single-tone fill with no gradient;
objects with no cast shadow;
text, numbers, letters, labels, signage lettering, watermark;
sparkles, glints, lens flare.

=== FINAL CHECK ===

Before finishing, confirm all of these:
- Everything from IMAGE 1 is still present, unchanged and in the same place.
- The half-built dome with its open rib skeleton and its crane is present in the lower left.
- The cutting machine is working down inside the existing ice pit, with cut ice blocks.
- The drill rig with its conveyor, ore hoppers and white rover is present in the upper right.
- There is no rail, tube, bridge or row of rings anywhere in the picture.
